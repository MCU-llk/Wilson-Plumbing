Plumbing and Electrical on Geyser Jobs

A geyser job often involves both plumbing (the cylinder, pipework and valves) and electrical work (the isolator, element and thermostat).

Wilson Plumbing & Electrical covers both sides. We fit the cylinder, pipework and safety valves, then connect the element, set the thermostat and test the electrical connection.
