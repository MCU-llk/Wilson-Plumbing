Specialized Synergy

Why Call One Team for Your Geyser?

A conventional plumber installs the tank and water pipes, then tells you to hire an electrician to connect the isolator and check the element. An electrician checks the breaker, but won't touch the leaking pressure valve or vacuum breakers.

Wilson Plumbing & Electrical solves both sides in a single appointment. We fit the cylinder, pipework, and safety valves, then immediately wire the element, set the thermostat, and certify the electrical connection.
